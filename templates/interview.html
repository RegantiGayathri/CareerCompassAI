💡 Interview Tips

Understand your resume completely before attending an interview.
Be ready to explain every project mentioned in your resume.
Practice DSA and SQL regularly for technical interviews.
Keep your answers clear, short and confident.
If you don't know an answer, be honest instead of guessing.
